Property Signals

Neighbourhood prices × local news, police incidents, planning and macro — lagged correlations and price forecasts.

Demo data. The prices and headlines loaded now are synthetic, generated for illustration. A news→price effect with a 4-month lag is deliberately built in so the analysis has something to find. Import your own data in Data & sources to analyse real prices.

Items

Sentiment runs from −1 (very negative for the area) to +1 (very positive). Edit a score to correct it; your edits are kept when re-scoring with the word list.

Cross-correlation by lag

Positive lag = the signal comes before the price change. Negative lag = prices move first (e.g. rising prices attracting positive coverage). Dashed lines: approx. 95% band, adjusted for overlapping windows.

Scatter at lag months

Each dot is one area-month (signal and price change each standardised within its area).

By neighbourhood

Correlation per area at selected lags. Few news items per area make single-area values noisy — trust the pooled row more.

Forecast

Median path with 10–90% range from 400 simulated paths. Model: pooled ridge regression on price momentum, city-wide momentum, policy-rate changes and lagged news, police and planning signals.

Backtest: does news improve the forecast?

Model trained on everything before the last 24 months, then asked to predict each of those months one step ahead.

What drives the model

Standardised coefficients: effect on next month's price change (percentage points) of a one-standard-deviation move in each input.

All neighbourhoods

Forecast change over the horizon, with 10–90% range.

This is a statistical model, not financial advice. It extrapolates relationships seen in past data; it cannot foresee shocks such as rule changes or a sudden rate move, and wide ranges are honest about that.

Where to get the data

This page can't fetch websites itself, so you download from these sources and drop the files above.

DataSourceHow
Prices per neighbourhoodSvensk MäklarstatistikMonthly kr/kvm for bostadsrätter by Stockholm area. Copy the figures into the price template below.
Individual salesBooli slutpriser, Hemnet såldaExport or copy sold listings with date, price, living area and area name. The importer computes kr/kvm and monthly medians.
National price indexSCB Statistikdatabasen, Valueguard HOXUse as a city-wide baseline (import as area "Stockholm").
News per areaGoogle News RSSOpen a feed for an area, save the page (Ctrl/Cmd+S) and drop it here with that area selected.
Police incidentspolisen.se/api/eventsFree open API, latest ~500 events. Save the JSON and drop it here; events are matched to areas by place names in the text. Import regularly to build up history.
Planning & infrastructureStockholm växer, Region Stockholm (tunnelbana)Add project milestones as news rows with category planning.
Policy rate, mortgage ratesRiksbanken, SCB (KPIF, unemployment)Monthly values in the macro template.
